Privacy Policy for OCC Advertising Console

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview

The OCC Advertising Console ("App") integrates with LinkedIn APIs to assist users in managing their advertising campaigns and organization pages.

2. Data Collection and Usage

OCC stores LinkedIn OAuth access tokens exclusively to authenticate API requests and manage the user's own ads, ad accounts, and company page settings on their behalf.

3. Prohibited Actions

OCC strictly does NOT engage in scraping LinkedIn member profiles, harvesting personal contact details, or reading private messages.

4. Data Storage and Security

All OAuth tokens are stored securely and are only used for direct interaction with official LinkedIn API endpoints authorized by the account holder.
